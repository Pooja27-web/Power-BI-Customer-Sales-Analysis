# Customer Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, payment methods, and geographic distribution.

The project demonstrates how business data can be transformed into interactive visualizations to support analytical exploration and data-driven decision-making.

## Project Overview

The report contains three pages:

- **Overview:** Key performance indicators and sales analysis.
- **Map:** Geographic exploration of sales and profitability.
- **Top Segments:** Analysis of customer segments and potential sales drivers.

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales Overview

The Overview page presents key performance indicators, including:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value

Additional visualizations explore:

- Sales by product category
- Monthly sales trends
- Orders by product category
- Top products by order volume
- Sales by payment method
- Customer segment distribution
- Orders by gender

Interactive filters support further exploration of the report.

### 2. Geographic Analysis

The Map page provides a geographic view of sales performance, allowing users to explore location-based patterns and compare business performance across regions.

### 3. Customer Segments & Key Influencers

The Top Segments page explores customer segments and potential factors associated with sales performance using Power BI visualizations.

The results should be interpreted in the context of the available data, selected outcome, and applied filters.
